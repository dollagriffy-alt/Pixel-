Chapter 1 · The Seventh Toll

RINGFALL

Juno Vey delivers noodles across the broken ring-city of Kessra, and every punch she throws rings like a bell. Stack seven tolls and the old stories say the sky cracks. Her grandfather says it's her that cracks. Eleven pages, read left to right.

Ringfall is an original comic in the shonen battle-manga tradition. Kessra, Juno, Brannoch, Ilex and the Hollow Choir are all new. Every page is drawn live in your browser with code, ink and screentone only.
