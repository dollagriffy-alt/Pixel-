# Pixel-

## Ringfall

`ringfall/chapter-01.html` is an original black-and-white battle manga chapter (11 pages), drawn procedurally on `<canvas>` with ink and screentone. Open it in a browser and read top to bottom, left to right. "Show script" lists every balloon, caption and sound effect under each page.

- **Juno Vey**: a noodle courier on the ring-city of Kessra. Every punch she throws "tolls" like a bell, and the echoes stack.
- **Brannoch**: her blind grandfather, a retired fighter who runs a noodle stall.
- **Ilex Morrow**: a Ring Authority enforcer whose Stillness Form soaks up vibration.
- **The Hollow Choir**: a colossal silent bell rising from the storm below the ring.

## Tamling Trails

`tamling-trails/index.html` is an original monster-taming RPG in a single file (no build, no assets; everything is drawn on `<canvas>`). Open it in a browser.

- **Chapter 1: Brackenford**: pick one of three starter Tamlings, train in the tall grass on Thistle Walk, catch wild Tamlings with Snare Charms, and beat Warden Hale in Cairn Hall for the Cairn Crest.
- **Controls**: arrows / WASD to move, Z / Space / Enter to confirm or talk, X / Esc to cancel or open the menu. On-screen buttons work on touch devices.
- Progress saves to the browser's local storage (menu → Save, or rest at the Hearth House).
